Questions or ideas? Write to me: cloudia.support@icloud.com

Please include your device model, iOS version and the Cloudia version (tap “Me” on the start screen; it’s at the bottom); a screenshot helps. I usually reply within three days.

FAQ

Why landscape only?
Most cloud photos are wide. Held sideways, the cloud fills the screen and there's room to draw.
No clouds nearby?
Tap “Find a cloud”, then pick “Today” or any of the built-in skies (more open up the more you draw).
After using Apple Pencil, my finger doesn't draw anymore?
That's to prevent stray marks: once you've used the Pencil, your finger moves, pinches and undoes by default. Turn on “Draw with fingers too” under “More” on the canvas.
Do my ★ follow me to a new phone or a reinstall?
Yes, as long as you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account: your ★ and everything you bought come along. Without iCloud, they're kept on this device only.
Do my drawings sync through iCloud?
Not automatically. Drawings live on this device and move to a new phone with iCloud Backup or device transfer. Deleting the app deletes them.
I paid, but the ★ didn't arrive?
Quit Cloudia completely and open it again; the app will credit any purchase it missed. If they still aren't there, write to me with your Apple receipt (Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → View Account → Purchase History). For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
The phone is on silent, but I still hear sound?
Only in “Shape a cloud”, and only if you allowed the microphone: to hear you blow, that screen's sound doesn't follow the silent switch. It goes back to normal when you leave.
I got a refund. Why did my ★ go down?
When a purchase is refunded, the ★ it added are taken back. If you'd already spent them, your balance goes below zero for a while, and the ★ you earn next fill it in first.
